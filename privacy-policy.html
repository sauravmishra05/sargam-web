Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 25, 2026 | Sargam Music

At Sargam Music, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our web application and Windows PWA handle user data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Sargam Music is built with privacy by design. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII), such as:

  • Your full name or address
  • Email addresses or passwords
  • Phone numbers or contacts
  • Precise GPS location
  • Financial or payment information

2. Anonymous Usage Data

To measure app performance and aggregate download statistics, Sargam Music collects minimal anonymous usage data, such as:

  • Aggregated download counters (FOSS vs. Google Cast build variants)
  • App version and OS platform (Windows / Android)
  • Anonymous app performance logs

3. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with third-party open music APIs (such as Audius) exclusively to stream audio tracks and fetch music metadata. No personal user identifiers are shared with third-party music providers.

4. Local Storage

Sargam Music uses browser LocalStorage solely to save your local app preferences (such as audio volume, theme preferences, and liked songs) directly on your device.
